## CRITICAL NEXT STEPS TO RANK ON GOOGLE

### 1. Google Business Profile (HIGHEST PRIORITY)
**This is THE most important factor for local search ranking!**

✅ Create/Claim Google Business Profiles for:
- Storm Lake location
- Cherokee location (if separate physical location)

**Go to:** https://www.google.com/business/

**Important:**
- Use consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across all profiles
- Business Name: "Cruz Electric"
- Categories: "Electrician", "Electrical Contractor", "Generator Installation Service"
- Service Areas: Add all cities mentioned (Storm Lake, Cherokee, Aurelia, Larrabee, etc.)
- Add all your services
- Upload photos of work, team, vehicles
- Post updates weekly (new jobs, tips, announcements)
- Respond to ALL reviews
